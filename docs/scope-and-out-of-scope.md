## 1. Scope

The SmartEdu platform covers the end-to-end digitization of course enrollment, attendance, assessment, and certification at a training center. The following functional areas form the project's main objectives and are included in the TO-BE solution:

Manage course, session, teacher, classroom, and student information in one centralized system.

Allow students to view available courses with OPEN status and vacant seats.

Enable students to enroll in courses online.

Automatically check course capacity during enrollment.

Automatically check student eligibility when a course has a prerequisite.

Prevent duplicate active enrollment by the same student in the same course instance.

Allow teachers to record attendance for their assigned courses/sessions.

Allow teachers to enter assessment results in the system.

Automatically calculate certificate eligibility from attendance percentage and passing score.

Automatically generate an e-certificate with a unique verification code when eligibility conditions are met.

Allow students to track their enrollment, attendance, result, and certificate status in one profile (self-service tracking).

Generate administrator reports on courses, students, results, and certificates.

Implement the main business rules (BR-01–BR-10) as validation logic within the system.

## 2. Out of Scope

The following areas will not be implemented in the current project phase. They may be considered in future phases through separate decisions and resource planning:

Payments, invoices, and financial operations (billing and payment gateway integration).

Development of a native iOS/Android mobile application.

Actual integration with third-party Learning Management Systems (LMS) or video-conferencing platforms (API-level design may be considered, but actual integration is excluded).

HR management, payroll, and teacher recruitment.

Physical infrastructure (classroom reservations and equipment management).

AI-based course recommendation engines or adaptive learning functionality.

Production and delivery of printed certificates.

Full migration of historical Excel-based data (legacy data) — only a conceptual approach may be shown; actual migration is outside the project scope.

Performance/load testing and actual deployment to production.

## 3. Note

The team developed the Scope and Out of Scope lists by analyzing the AS-IS scenario, identifying stakeholder needs, and detailing the TO-BE solution. Additional requirements or constraints identified during implementation will be documented separately under Assumptions and Open Questions.
